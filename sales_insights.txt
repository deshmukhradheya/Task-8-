
Sales Insights

1. The West region had the highest total sales across all months, especially peaking in November and December, indicating strong end-of-year performance.

2. Electronics emerged as the top-performing product category, consistently leading in monthly sales and contributing significantly to overall revenue.

3. Sales volume showed a steady increase from Q2 to Q4, suggesting seasonal or promotional influence in the latter half of the year.

4. Beauty and Clothing categories had the lowest average sales, indicating potential areas for marketing improvement or product reevaluation.
